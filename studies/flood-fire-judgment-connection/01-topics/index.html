    Topics Research¶

    Question¶

    What is the biblical connection between the flood of Noah and the final fire judgment? Does Scripture itself make this connection explicitly?

    FLOOD — GENERAL¶

    • Gen 6:17
    • Gen 7:6-7
    • Gen 7:10-12
    • Gen 7:17
    • Gen 9:11
    • Gen 9:15
    • Gen 9:28
    • Gen 10:1
    • Gen 10:32
    • Gen 11:10
    • Psa 29:10
    • Mat 24:38-39

    FLOOD — References to the deluge of Noah. Gen 6:17; 7:6-7,10-12,17; 9:11,15,28; 10:1,32; 11:10; Psa 29:10; Mat 24:38-39.

    NOAH — GENERAL¶

    • Gen 5:29-32
    • Gen 6:8-22
    • Gen 7:1-24
    • Gen 8:1-22
    • Gen 9:1-29
    • Gen 10:1
    • 1 Chr 1:4
    • Isa 54:9
    • Eze 14:14,20
    • Mat 24:37-38
    • Luk 17:26-27
    • Heb 11:7
    • 1 Pet 3:20
    • 2 Pet 2:5

    NOAH — Son of Lamech; righteous man who found grace in the eyes of the LORD. Built the ark, preserved through the flood. Gen 5:29-32; 6:8-22; 7:1-24; 8:1-22; 9:1-29; 10:1; 1 Chr 1:4; Isa 54:9; Eze 14:14,20; Mat 24:37-38; Luk 17:26-27; Heb 11:7; 1 Pet 3:20; 2 Pet 2:5.

    FIRE — FIGURATIVE/JUDGMENTS¶

    • Deut 4:24
    • Deut 32:22
    • 2 Sam 22:13
    • Psa 18:8
    • Psa 50:3
    • Psa 97:3
    • Isa 33:14
    • Isa 66:15-16
    • Dan 7:9-10
    • Mal 3:2-3
    • Mal 4:1
    • Mat 3:11-12
    • Mat 25:41
    • Luk 12:49
    • 2 Thess 1:7-8
    • Heb 12:29
    • 2 Pet 3:7,10-12
    • Rev 20:9-10

    FIRE — As instrument of divine judgment: God is a consuming fire (Deut 4:24; Heb 12:29). Fire goes before the LORD (Psa 97:3). Eschatological fire judgment (Mal 4:1; 2 Pet 3:7,10-12; Rev 20:9). Fire and brimstone on Sodom (Gen 19:24). Everlasting fire (Mat 25:41; Jude 1:7).

    JUDGMENT — THE GENERAL¶

    • Ecc 12:14
    • Mat 10:15
    • Mat 12:36
    • Mat 25:31-46
    • Acts 17:31
    • Rom 2:16
    • Rom 14:10
    • 2 Cor 5:10
    • 2 Pet 2:4,9
    • 2 Pet 3:7
    • Jude 1:6,14-15
    • Rev 20:11-15

    JUDGMENT — The final judgment of all humanity. God will judge the secrets of men (Rom 2:16). The Lord knows how to reserve the unjust unto the day of judgment (2 Pet 2:9). Reserved unto fire against the day of judgment (2 Pet 3:7). Dead judged according to their works (Rev 20:12-13).

    SODOM — DESTRUCTION¶

    • Gen 19:1-29
    • Deut 29:23
    • Isa 1:9-10
    • Isa 13:19
    • Jer 49:18
    • Lam 4:6
    • Eze 16:49-50
    • Amos 4:11
    • Mat 10:15
    • Mat 11:24
    • Luk 10:12
    • Luk 17:29
    • Rom 9:29
    • 2 Pet 2:6
    • Jude 1:7

    SODOM — Destroyed by fire and brimstone from the LORD out of heaven (Gen 19:24-25). Used as paradigm of divine judgment throughout Scripture. Peter makes it an ensample (hupodeigma) to those that after should live ungodly (2 Pet 2:6). Jude says it is set forth for an example suffering the vengeance of eternal fire (Jude 1:7). Jesus pairs it with the flood as type of His coming (Luk 17:29-30).

    EARTH — DESTRUCTION OF¶

    • Isa 24:19-20
    • Isa 34:4
    • Isa 51:6
    • Zeph 3:8
    • Mat 24:35
    • Heb 1:10-12
    • 2 Pet 3:7,10-12
    • Rev 6:14
    • Rev 20:11
    • Rev 21:1

    EARTH, DESTRUCTION OF — The heavens shall vanish away like smoke, the earth shall wax old like a garment (Isa 51:6). Elements shall melt with fervent heat (2 Pet 3:10,12). Earth and heaven fled away from the great white throne (Rev 20:11). First heaven and first earth passed away (Rev 21:1).

    EARTH — A NEW EARTH¶

    • Isa 65:17
    • Isa 66:22
    • 2 Pet 3:13
    • Rev 21:1

    EARTH, A NEW — I create new heavens and a new earth (Isa 65:17). The new heavens and new earth shall remain before me (Isa 66:22). We look for new heavens and a new earth wherein dwelleth righteousness (2 Pet 3:13). I saw a new heaven and a new earth (Rev 21:1).

    CREATION — GENERAL¶

    • Gen 1:1-31
    • Gen 2:1-3
    • Psa 33:6,9
    • Psa 102:25
    • Isa 40:26
    • Isa 42:5
    • Isa 45:18
    • John 1:1-3
    • Heb 11:3
    • 2 Pet 3:5

    CREATION — In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth (Gen 1:1). By the word of the LORD were the heavens made (Psa 33:6). By the word of God the heavens were of old (2 Pet 3:5). Through faith we understand that the worlds were framed by the word of God (Heb 11:3).

    RESTORATION — OF ALL THINGS¶

    • Acts 3:21
    • Rev 21:5

    RESTORATION OF ALL THINGS — The times of restitution of all things, which God hath spoken by the mouth of all his holy prophets (Acts 3:21). Behold, I make all things new (Rev 21:5).

    REFINING — FIGURATIVE¶

    • Isa 1:25
    • Isa 48:10
    • Mal 3:2-3
    • 1 Cor 3:13-15
    • 1 Pet 1:7

    REFINING — He is like a refiner's fire (Mal 3:2). He shall purify the sons of Levi, and purge them as gold and silver (Mal 3:3). I have refined thee but not with silver; I have chosen thee in the furnace of affliction (Isa 48:10). Every man's work shall be revealed by fire (1 Cor 3:13).

    COVENANT — GENERAL¶

    • Gen 9:8-17
    • Gen 15:18
    • Gen 17:1-14
    • Exo 19:5
    • Deut 5:2-3
    • Jer 31:31-34
    • Heb 8:8-13

    COVENANT — Noahic covenant: neither shall all flesh be cut off any more by the waters of a flood; neither shall there any more be a flood to destroy the earth (Gen 9:11). Rainbow as token of the covenant (Gen 9:12-16).

    RAINBOW — GENERAL¶

    • Gen 9:13-16
    • Eze 1:28
    • Rev 4:3
    • Rev 10:1

    RAINBOW — I do set my bow in the cloud, and it shall be for a token of a covenant between me and the earth (Gen 9:13). God's promise: the waters shall no more become a flood to destroy all flesh (Gen 9:15). Everlasting covenant (Gen 9:16).

    SCOFFING — GENERAL¶

    • 2 Pet 3:3-4
    • Jude 1:18
    • Psa 1:1
    • Pro 1:22
    • Pro 9:7-8
    • Pro 14:6
    • Isa 28:14-22

    SCOFFING — There shall come in the last days scoffers, walking after their own lusts, and saying, Where is the promise of his coming? for since the fathers fell asleep, all things continue as they were from the beginning of the creation (2 Pet 3:3-4). Mockers in the last time who should walk after their own ungodly lusts (Jude 1:18).

    PURIFICATION — GENERAL¶

    • Num 31:23
    • Mal 3:2-3
    • Isa 1:25
    • Isa 48:10
    • 1 Pet 1:7
    • Rev 3:18

    PURIFICATION — Every thing that may abide the fire, ye shall make it go through the fire, and it shall be clean (Num 31:23). He is like a refiner's fire; he shall purify the sons of Levi (Mal 3:2-3). The trial of your faith, though it be tried with fire (1 Pet 1:7).
