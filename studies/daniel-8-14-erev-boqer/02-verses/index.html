    Verses¶

    Question¶

    In Daniel 8:14, did Daniel intentionally use 'erev boqer' (evening-morning) instead of his usual 'yamim' (days) as a deliberate fingerprint of concealment for the time period — encodable because a day-for-year decoder already existed in Numbers 14:34 and Ezekiel 4:6 — with Gabriel's command to 'seal the vision' (Daniel 8:26) confirming the concealment is intentional? And is the resulting years-reading confirmed (with Daniel 9's 'chathak' the strongest confirmation, not the sole proof)?

    The tamid taken away; the 'how long' question about the daily, the transgression, and the trampled sanctuary¶

    Yea, he magnified [himself] even to the prince of the host, and by him the daily [sacrifice] was taken away, and the place of his sanctuary was cast down. And an host was given [him] against the daily [sacrifice] by reason of transgression, and it cast down the truth to the ground; and it practised, and prospered. Then I heard one saint speaking, and another saint said unto that certain [saint] which spake, How long [shall be] the vision [concerning] the daily [sacrifice], and the transgression of desolation, to give both the sanctuary and the host to be trodden under foot?

    Context: Daniel 8, the ram-and-he-goat vision. The little horn removes 'the daily' (ha-tamid) and casts down the sanctuary place. 8:13 poses the 'ad-matay' ('how long') question that 8:14 answers. The question's subject is the tamid + transgression + trampled qodesh/tsava — grounding the tamid-primary candidate: the answer may count in the tamid's own evening-morning units.

    Notes: Parser (8:13): 'ha-tamid' = Noun.ms.Abs with article (lemma tamid, H8548), 'ha-chazon' Noun.ms.Abs (the vision, lemma chazon H2377), 'qodesh' + 'tsava' (holiness + service/host) both Noun.ms.Abs joined by waw = the compound object trampled. KJV supplies '[sacrifice]' in italics — the Hebrew is the bare substantive 'the tamid.' The question is literally 'until when the vision — the tamid and the transgression...' The tamid is thus the vision's subject, and it is an evening+morning service (see Ex 29:38-42; Num 28:3-8), the datum behind the tamid-primary reading. 8:11 'huram/huwsar' relates to removal of the daily (Hophal). Distinct from concealment: nothing here is sealed; this is the open problem the 2300 answers.

    CRUX — 'Unto 2300 evening-morning; then the sanctuary shall be nitsdaq'¶

    And he said unto me, Unto two thousand and three hundred days; then shall the sanctuary be cleansed.

    Context: The answer to 8:13's 'how long.' KJV renders the counted unit 'days,' but the Hebrew has neither yom/yamim nor any conjunction: 'ad erev boqer 'alpayim ushlosh me'oth — 'unto evening morning two thousand and three hundred.' Then 'we-nitsdaq qodesh' — 'and the sanctuary/holiness shall be vindicated/set right.' This is the single-cardinal-scoping-the-fused-pair crux.

The terminus EVENT (sanctuary vindicated) is stated openly here — it is NOT the sealed element (contrast 8:26).

    Daniel seeks the meaning; Gabriel: 'Understand, O son of man; at the time of the end shall be the vision'¶

    And it came to pass, when I, [even] I Daniel, had seen the vision, and sought for the meaning, then, behold, there stood before me as the appearance of a man. ... So he came near where I stood: and when he came, I was afraid, and fell upon my face: but he said unto me, Understand, O son of man: for at the time of the end [shall be] the vision. ... And he said, Behold, I will make thee know what shall be in the last end of the indignation: for at the time appointed the end [shall be].

    Context: Daniel 8:15-19. Daniel sought the meaning of the vision (mareh/chazon). Gabriel is commissioned to make Daniel understand. 8:17 'haben ben-adam' ('understand, O son of man') and 'at the time of the end shall be the vision (chazon)'; 8:19 'at the time appointed the end shall be.' Establishes the end-time horizon that motivates sealing and the biyn (understand) chain that reaches to 9:23.

    Notes: 8:15 'mareh' (appearance of a man) uses H4758 mareh. 8:17 Gabriel's vocative 'ben-adam' (son of man) parallels the angelic address in ch. 9; the imperative 'haben' (understand, from biyn) begins the understanding-chain resumed at 9:23 ('consider the mareh'). 8:17/8:19 'et qets' (time of the end) is the register co-text the apocalyptic-time-unit grammar entry flags as accompanying the sealed span. The chazon is placed at the 'et qets — the distant horizon that grounds the 'for many days' sealing rationale of 8:26.

    CRUX — 'The mareh of the evening and the morning is true; shut thou up (satham) the vision, for it is for many days (yamim)'¶

    And the vision of the evening and the morning which was told [is] true: wherefore shut thou up the vision; for it [shall be] for many days.

    Context: Gabriel's closing certification. Three load-bearing data in one verse: (1) the time element is re-cited as 'the evening and the morning' now ARTICULATED and CONJOINED (ha-erev we-ha-boqer) — anaphoric back-reference, contrast with the bare asyndetic 8:14 form; (2) 'setom he-chazon' — satham (H5640) 'shut up' the vision, the direct concealment label; (3) 'le-yamim rabbim' — yamim ('many days') used in the very sealing verse, the negative control proving Daniel had and used yamim in this vision-complex.

    Daniel sick and astonished at the vision; none understood it¶

    And I Daniel fainted, and was sick [certain] days; afterward I rose up, and did the king's business; and I was astonished at the vision, but none understood [it].

    Context: Daniel 8:27, the chapter's close. Even after Gabriel's explanation, the one element left unexplained (the 2300 time period) leaves Daniel astonished at the vision (mareh) and 'none understood it' — the unresolved state that Dan 9:23 ('consider the mareh') resumes. Confirms the time element remained opaque at the end of ch. 8.

    Notes: 'was astonished at the mareh (H4758)... none understood (biyn) it.' Establishes that the 2300 mareh was NOT understood at delivery — the precondition for the sealed-until-later economy and for Dan 9 continuing the explanation. Supports the 'unreadable when given' half of the thesis, from the text: the incomprehension is stated, not merely inferred.

    The 8-to-9 bridge: 'Understand the matter, and consider the mareh'¶

    At the beginning of thy supplications the commandment came forth, and I am come to shew [thee]; for thou [art] greatly beloved: therefore understand the matter, and consider the vision.

    Context: Daniel 9:23. Gabriel (the same interpreter as ch. 8) returns and tells Daniel to 'understand (biyn) the matter and consider the mareh.' KJV renders mareh here 'the vision,' but the Hebrew is mareh (H4758) — the same word 8:26 used for the erev-boqer TIME element, not chazon (the broad vision). This is the linguistic warrant that Dan 9 resumes the ONE unexplained item of ch. 8: the time period.

    Notes: The word behind 'consider the vision' is mareh (H4758) per the H4758 library entry (Dan 9:23 listed there). biyn ('understand') links 8:16-17 -> 9:23 (the understanding-chain). Since 8:26 attached mareh specifically to 'the evening and the morning' (the time element), and 8:27 left it not understood, 9:23's 'consider the mareh' sends Daniel back to the time period. This is the bridge that lets Dan 9's chathak (9:24) function as continuation/confirmation of the 8:14 count. NOTE this is an inference from the shared mareh vocabulary, not an explicit cross-reference in the text.

    CRUX — 'Seventy weeks are nechtak (chathak, hapax) upon thy people... to seal up (lachtom, chatham) the vision'¶

    Seventy weeks are determined upon thy people and upon thy holy city, to finish the transgression, and to make an end of sins, and to make reconciliation for iniquity, and to bring in everlasting righteousness, and to seal up the vision and prophecy, and to anoint the most Holy.

    Context: Daniel 9:24, the seventy-weeks decree. Two seal/cut words that must be kept distinct: (1) 'nechtak' = chathak (H2852, Hebrew hapax) 'are determined/cut off,' the main verb; (2) 'lachtom' = chatham (H2856) 'to seal up' the vision and prophecy — one of six positive accomplishments (finish transgression, end sins, reconcile iniquity, bring everlasting righteousness, seal up vision/prophecy, anoint most holy). The sealing here is AUTHENTICATION by fulfillment, not concealment.

    Context: Jeremiah 32:10-14. A sealed purchase-deed (chatham) preserved in an earthen vessel 'that they may continue many days.' The clearest picture of sealing as PRESERVATION/AUTHENTICATION of a document for the future — the counter-reading to pure concealment. Note 'continue many days' echoes Dan 8:26 'for many days.'

    Notes: Jer 32:10-14 chatham (H2856) = seal a legal deed for authentication + preservation ('continue many days'). This is the paradigm of chatham = secure/authenticate a document — the sense chatham carries at Dan 9:24 (authenticate the vision by fulfillment) and Dan 12:4 ('seal the book'). Strong counter-datum to reading all Daniel sealing as concealment: chatham = preserve/authenticate. Reinforces the satham(conceal)/chatham(authenticate) distinction (focus 3).     'Write the vision... for the vision is yet for an appointed time... it will surely come'¶

    And the LORD answered me, and said, Write the vision, and make [it] plain upon tables, that he may run that readeth it. For the vision [is] yet for an appointed time, but at the end it shall speak, and not lie: though it tarry, wait for it; because it will surely come, it will not tarry.

    Context: Habakkuk 2:2-3. 'Write the vision (chazon)... for it is yet for an appointed time (moed)... at the end it shall speak.' A vision recorded and preserved for an appointed future time — the sealed-for-appointed-time frame that parallels Dan 8:26 ('for many days') and 8:17,19 ('the time of the end').     Notes: Hab 2:2-3 chazon (H2377) 'written plain... for an appointed time (moed)... at the end it shall speak.'
