# Cross-Testament Parallels

- Dan 8:14 → Gen 1:5 (ot, shared lexemes erev + boqer (H6153 + H1242) in the same evening-first order; contrast in syntax (Gen 1 joins them with waw and pairs with yom; Dan 8:14 bare asyndetic, no yom))
- Dan 8:14 → Dan 12:11-12 (ot, in-corpus contrast — same book/genre apocalyptic durations; 12:11-12 use yamim (H3117), 8:14 avoids it (the switch tell))
- Dan 8:26 → Dan 12:4 (ot, shared satham + chatham (H5640 + H2856) sealing vocabulary and 'time of the end' (et qets) register co-text)
- Dan 8:26 → Isa 29:11 (ot, sealed-vision / none-can-read motif (chatham); predates Daniel — tests concealment vs genre-convention)
- Dan 8:26 → Jer 32:10-14 (ot, sealing (chatham) a document preserved for 'many days' — the preservation/authentication counter-reading; verbal echo 'continue many days' / 'for many days')
- Dan 8:26 → Rev 22:10 (nt, deliberate inversion — Daniel 'seal to the time of the end' vs John 'seal NOT, the time is at hand'; confirms sealing = time-bounded withholding)
- Dan 9:24 → Dan 8:26 (ot, mareh (H4758) bridge — 9:23 'consider the mareh' resumes the 8:26 mareh (time element); links the 490 explanation to the 2300)
- Dan 9:24 → Dan 9:26-27 (ot, cut/determine verb contrast — 9:24 chathak (H2852, hapax) vs 9:26-27 charats (H2782, Daniel's ordinary decree-verb) and karath (H3772); the available-but-unchosen charats marks chathak's selection)
- Dan 8:14 → Ex 29:38-42 (ot, tamid evening/morning service (the topic of 8:13's question); ORDER contrast — tamid is morning-first, Dan 8:14 evening-first)
