# Cross-Testament Parallels

- Luke 10:19 → Deut 8:15 (ot, vocabulary (scorpion, serpent))
- Luke 10:19 → Hab 3:19 (ot, vocabulary + concept (power, tread))
- Luke 10:19 → Rev 2:26 (nt, vocabulary (authority) — authority given to overcomers)
- Luke 10:19 → Eph 6:11 (nt, thematic — standing against the enemy's power)
- John 15:5 → Ezek 15:2 (ot, vocabulary + structural (vine — OT vine metaphor))
- John 15:5 → John 15:4 (nt, immediate context — branch cannot bear fruit of itself)
- Eph 6:10 → Exod 15:2 (ot, vocabulary (strength — the LORD is my strength))
- Eph 6:10 → Psa 20:7 (ot, vocabulary + concept (strength — some trust in chariots))
- Eph 6:10 → 2 Cor 12:9 (nt, thematic — divine power through human weakness)
- Eph 6:10 → Eph 1:19 (nt, vocabulary — identical power vocabulary (kratos, ischus))
- 2 Cor 12:9 → Prov 3:34 (ot, vocabulary (grace) + thematic (grace to the humble))
- 2 Cor 12:9 → 2 Cor 12:10 (nt, immediate context — when weak then strong)
- 2 Cor 12:9 → 1 Cor 15:10 (nt, thematic (grace as power source) + 'yet not I' pattern)
- Acts 3:16 → Psa 33:21 (ot, vocabulary (name, trust) — trust in His holy name)
- Acts 3:16 → 1 Pet 1:21 (nt, thematic (faith through/in God))
- Acts 3:16 → Acts 4:10 (nt, vocabulary (name of Jesus Christ) — Peter's consistent attribution)
- Matt 28:18 → Dan 7:27 (ot, vocabulary + concept (authority, heaven — universal dominion))
- Matt 28:18 → John 19:11 (nt, vocabulary (authority, given) — all authority from above)
- Matt 28:18 → Rev 12:10 (nt, vocabulary (authority, heaven) — authority of Christ)
