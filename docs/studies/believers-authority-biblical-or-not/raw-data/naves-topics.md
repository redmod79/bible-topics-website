# Raw Nave's Topic Data

## DEMONS
Power over, given the disciples: MAT 10:1; MRK 6:7; 16:17. Cast out by disciples MRK 9:38; LUK 10:17; Peter ACT 5:16; Paul ACT 16:16-18; 19:12; Philip ACT 8:7. Disciples could not expel MRK 9:18,28,29. Sceva's sons ACT 19:13-16.

## MIRACLES — Miraculous Gifts of the Holy Spirit
Performed through the power of God JHN 3:2; ACT 14:3; 15:12; 19:11. Of the Holy Spirit MAT 12:28; ROM 15:19; 1CO 12:9,10,28,30. In the name of Christ MRK 16:17; ACT 3:16; 4:30. Faith required MAT 17:20; 21:21; JHN 14:12; ACT 3:16; 6:8. Power to work given the disciples MRK 3:14,15; 16:17,18,20.

## HOLY SPIRIT
General scriptures covering Spirit's work: empowerment, filling, guidance, gifts. Key verses: ACT 1:8; 1CO 12:3-11; EPH 5:18; GAL 5:22-23.

## PRAYER — Conditions and Answers
MRK 9:28,29; MAT 17:20; JHN 14:13-14; JHN 15:7; JAS 5:16. Faith required.

## FASTING
Accompanied by prayer DAN 9:3. MAT 17:21; MRK 9:29; ACT 13:3; 14:23.

## HUMILITY
PRO 3:34; JAS 4:6,10; 1PE 5:5,6. Exemplified: ACT 3:12; 1CO 15:9,10; 2CO 3:5; 12:7.

## APOSTLES — Commission and Power
Miraculous power given to MAT 10:1; MRK 3:15; 6:7; 16:17; LUK 9:1,2; 10:9,17.

## VINE
Symbolical JHN 15:1-5.

## GIFTS FROM GOD

## DISCIPLESHIP
